Notes

This tracker follows up on my undergraduate thesis (TCC) on oil and the Colombian economy. The question it keeps asking: when Brent moves, how much of that passes through to the peso and to the real? Colombia relies far more on crude for its export revenue than Brazil does, so the peso should track oil more closely.

The correlation figure under each indexed chart uses weekly log changes of the currency's USD value against weekly log changes of Brent, over the selected range. A positive number means the currency tends to strengthen in weeks when oil rises. Weekly changes are used because the TRM for a given day is set from the previous day's trading.
